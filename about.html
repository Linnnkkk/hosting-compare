About HostingPick

Our Mission

HostingPick exists to cut through the noise of web hosting marketing. We buy real hosting accounts, run standardized tests, and report what we actually find — not what hosting companies want you to hear.

How We Test

Every hosting provider on our site goes through the same testing process:

  • We buy accounts anonymously — providers don't know they're being reviewed
  • Uptime monitoring — 24/7 checks for 6+ months via third-party monitoring
  • Speed benchmarks — Time to First Byte (TTFB) from 10 global locations
  • Load testing — Gradual ramp to 500 concurrent visitors
  • Support testing — We submit real tickets and time responses
  • Pricing transparency — We show promo AND renewal pricing

Editorial Independence

Our rankings are based solely on test results and hands-on experience. Affiliate commissions do not influence our ratings, rankings, or reviews. A host that pays us more will not rank higher.

Affiliate Disclosure: HostingPick earns commissions when you click links and purchase hosting. This is how we fund our testing. It does not affect our rankings. We would rather earn your trust than a quick commission.

What We Cover

  • Shared hosting for beginners and small businesses
  • WordPress-optimized hosting
  • Managed WordPress hosting
  • Cloud hosting and VPS
  • Budget hosting under $3-5/month

Update Schedule

We re-test hosting providers quarterly and update our reviews when:

  • Pricing changes significantly
  • Major feature updates are released
  • Uptime or speed patterns shift
  • A provider makes significant support changes
