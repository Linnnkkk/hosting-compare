Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to HostingPick ("we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website at https://hostingpick.liiink.site/.

Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring website address
  • Pages viewed and time spent on pages
  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

Information You Provide

We do not currently collect personal information through forms, accounts, or user submissions.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Essential cookies: Required for basic website functionality
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Advertising cookies: Third-party advertising services may use cookies to display relevant ads
  • Affiliate tracking cookies: When you click links to hosting providers, affiliate cookies may be set to track referrals

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect your browsing experience.

Affiliate Links Disclosure

HostingPick contains affiliate links to web hosting providers. When you click an affiliate link and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. These affiliate relationships do not influence our rankings, reviews, or editorial content. Our reviews are based on independent testing and analysis.

When you click an affiliate link, the following may occur:

  • A cookie is placed on your browser by the hosting provider or affiliate network
  • Your click and/or purchase may be tracked for commission purposes
  • The hosting provider may collect personal information as part of their sign-up process

Third-Party Services

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to track and analyze website traffic. Google Analytics collects information anonymously. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense

We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. Opt out at Google Ads Settings.

GitHub Pages

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Hosting Provider Websites

When you click links to hosting providers, you are leaving our website. Each hosting provider has its own privacy policy governing how they collect and use your data. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Analytics data is retained by Google according to their policies. Affiliate cookies are governed by the respective affiliate programs' policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data held about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes are indicated by a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us
