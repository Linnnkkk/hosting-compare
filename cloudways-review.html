Performance

Cloudways delivered 267ms TTFB in our tests — second only to WP Engine (198ms) among our reviewed hosts. The difference is Cloudways gives you cloud-level infrastructure at a fraction of the cost.

Built-in Redis and Memcached caching, free Cloudflare CDN, and PHP 8.x optimization all contribute to excellent speed. You can also choose your server location from 60+ data centers worldwide.

Who Should Use Cloudways?

Best for: Developers, agencies, and technically-minded users who want cloud speed and control without managing server infrastructure. Great for hosting multiple client sites or resource-heavy applications.
Not ideal for: Complete beginners who want a simple dashboard and phone support. If terms like SSH, Redis, and Git sound intimidating, Hostinger or Bluehost are better choices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cloudways better than shared hosting?

For performance, yes — significantly. Cloud servers outperform shared hosting by a wide margin. But Cloudways requires more technical knowledge and costs more than budget shared hosting.

Can I use Cloudways for email?

No. Cloudways doesn't offer email hosting. You'll need a separate email provider like Google Workspace, Zoho Mail, or your domain registrar's email service.

How does Cloudways compare to WP Engine?

WP Engine is WordPress-only with premium support and features. Cloudways supports multiple applications (WordPress, Laravel, Magento) and gives you more server control, but less WordPress-specific optimization.
