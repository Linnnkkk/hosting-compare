Performance

SiteGround consistently ranks as one of the fastest shared hosting providers. Their performance edge comes from several key technologies:

Google Cloud Infrastructure

All SiteGround shared hosting plans run on Google Cloud Platform. This means your site benefits from Google's premium-tier network and data centers across multiple continents. You can choose your server location during signup (US, Europe, Asia, or Australia).

Ultrafast PHP

SiteGround implements a custom PHP execution engine that delivers significantly faster page loads than standard PHP. In our tests, Ultrafast PHP reduced TTFB by up to 30% compared to their already-fast standard PHP setup.

SG Optimizer Plugin

The free SG Optimizer plugin handles front-end optimization automatically: image lazy loading, CSS/JS minification, HTML optimization, and dynamic caching. It integrates directly with SiteGround's server-level caching for maximum performance.

Free Cloudflare CDN

Every plan includes SiteGround's custom Cloudflare CDN integration. Static assets are served from 300+ global edge locations, dramatically reducing load times for visitors far from your origin server.

Our Test Results: Average TTFB of 312ms across 10 global locations. Load time for a default WordPress install: 1.2 seconds. These are the best numbers we've seen from any shared host.

WordPress Features

SiteGround is one of only three hosts officially recommended by WordPress.org, and for good reason. Their WordPress toolset is comprehensive:

  • 1-Click WordPress Install — Set up a new WordPress site in under 60 seconds through SiteGround's custom dashboard.
  • Auto-Updates — Automatically update WordPress core, plugins, and themes. You control the schedule.
  • Staging Environment (GrowBig and above) — Clone your live site to a staging URL, test changes, then push back to production with one click.
  • WP-CLI — Full WP-CLI access for advanced WordPress management from the command line.
  • WordPress Starter — Pre-configured setups for popular site types (blog, portfolio, WooCommerce store) with recommended plugins and themes.
  • Free Site Migration — SiteGround's WordPress migrator plugin transfers your existing site from any host at no extra charge.

Customer Support

SiteGround's support is widely regarded as the best in shared hosting. We tested it ourselves and here's what we found:

Support Channels

  • Live Chat — Available 24/7 on all plans. Average wait time in our tests: under 2 minutes.
  • Tickets — Detailed technical issues get thorough, well-written responses. Average first response: 10-15 minutes.
  • Phone — Available on GrowBig and GoGeek plans only.

Support Quality

In our mystery shopper tests, SiteGround support agents correctly resolved 9 out of 10 issues on first contact. They demonstrate genuine WordPress expertise, not just scripted responses. This is a significant step above most competitors.

Knowledge Base

SiteGround maintains an extensive library of tutorials and guides. Most common questions are answered in detail with step-by-step instructions and screenshots.

Security

SiteGround takes a multi-layered approach to security that goes well beyond the basics:

AI Anti-Bot System

SiteGround's proprietary AI system monitors traffic patterns and automatically blocks malicious bots before they can overwhelm your site. This protects against brute-force attacks and credential stuffing.

Free SSL Certificates

Let's Encrypt SSL certificates are installed automatically for every site. Wildcard SSL is also supported at no extra cost.

Daily Backups

Up to 30 days of daily backups are included on all plans. GrowBig and GoGeek plans also get on-demand backup creation with one-click restore.

Server-Level Firewall

A custom WAF (Web Application Firewall) runs at the server level, filtering malicious requests before they reach your WordPress installation. Rules are continuously updated based on the latest threat intelligence.

Who Should Use SiteGround?

SiteGround is the right choice if you value performance and support above rock-bottom pricing:

  • WordPress site owners who want the best possible speed and reliability from shared hosting.
  • Small businesses that depend on their website and need support they can count on.
  • Bloggers and creators who want a hassle-free hosting experience with automatic updates and backups.
  • Freelancers and agencies managing multiple client sites (GoGeek plan with white-label options).
  • Anyone migrating from a slower or less reliable host who wants a noticeable improvement.
Not ideal for: Strictly budget-conscious users who plan to host for many years. The renewal price of $17.99/mo is significantly higher than Hostinger ($11.99/mo) or DreamHost ($7.99/mo). See our best budget hosting page for cheaper alternatives.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SiteGround worth the renewal price?

It depends on your priorities. If speed and support quality matter most to you, SiteGround delivers value that justifies the cost. However, if you're on a tight budget, the jump from $2.99 to $17.99/mo at renewal is steep. Consider negotiating a discount with their retention team or switching to a longer billing cycle.

Can I host multiple websites on SiteGround?

The StartUp plan supports only one website. For multiple sites, you'll need the GrowBig plan ($4.99/mo promo) or GoGeek ($7.99/mo promo), both of which allow unlimited websites.

Does SiteGround offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. SiteGround offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all shared hosting plans. If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, you can request a full refund.

How does SiteGround compare to Hostinger?

SiteGround offers better raw performance (312ms vs 386ms TTFB) and superior support. Hostinger is significantly cheaper, especially at renewal ($11.99 vs $17.99/mo), and includes more storage. For most beginners, Hostinger offers better value. For WordPress-focused sites where speed matters, SiteGround is worth the premium. See our Hostinger review for a detailed comparison.
